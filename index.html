LinkedIn Positioning Review

Prepared for Robert Casinover. A clearer way to connect your founder story, The Build, AI-driven procurement and your voice in the trades.

The biggest opportunity is not adding more to the profile. It is making the existing story easier to understand in one pass.
01 / What I found

The product story is stronger than the profile currently makes it feel.

Your public company material gives a visitor a concrete picture of the problem, the workflow and the audience. Your personal profile has the ingredients, but they are spread across several narratives.

01

Lead with the buyer problem

The Build has a concrete contractor promise: simplify project operations and get competitive material bids back quickly. That is easier to understand than a broad ConTech label.

02

Make AI the mechanism

AI-driven procurement is a distinctive part of your current direction. It should support the value proposition, not compete with it.

03

Turn the podcast into proof

Pipe Dreams to Profits gives you an ongoing public platform around trades, distribution, technology and business. It should reinforce your founder positioning rather than sit beside it.

02 / Your current positioning

Founder, finance operator, construction-tech builder.

What is already clear

You are building The Build, focused on construction procurement and automation, while bringing deep finance and operating experience into the trades.

What is still unclear

Who should feel most directly addressed when they land on your profile, and what specific transformation should they associate with you first.

A visitor should understand the problem you are attacking before they have to understand every part of your background.
03 / Headline

Your current headline

Founder & CEO, The Build | Automating Construction Procurement with AI | Host, Pipe Dreams to Profits | ConTech + Trades
Why it can be stronger

Clarity before completeness.

The current line tries to carry founder role, AI, podcast, ConTech and trades in one place. None of those are wrong. The issue is hierarchy.

What it says

Founder and CEO of The Build, AI procurement, podcast host, ConTech and trades.

What it misses

A fast answer to the buyer question: who is The Build for, and what does it make easier?

Why it matters

Someone arriving from a post, podcast or referral should not need to assemble the connection between your content and the product.

Five stronger directions
01
Founder & CEO, The Build | Automating Construction Procurement with AI | Helping Contractors Source, Order & Manage Materials
Makes the audience and practical procurement value clearer while keeping the founder role.
02
Founder & CEO, The Build | AI-Powered Construction Procurement | Connecting Contractors, Materials & Distributors
Connects the product, two-sided marketplace and construction audience in one line.
03
Building AI-Powered Procurement for Contractors | Founder & CEO, The Build | Host, Pipe Dreams to Profits
Leans into the founder thesis and keeps the podcast as a credibility layer, not the lead.
04
Founder & CEO, The Build | Making Construction Procurement Simpler, Faster & More Automated | ConTech + Trades
Uses a broad but concrete outcome frame that fits both the product and founder story.
05
Construction Procurement, Reimagined with AI | Founder & CEO, The Build | Contractor + Distributor Technology
Positions AI procurement as the category, with The Build as the vehicle behind it.
